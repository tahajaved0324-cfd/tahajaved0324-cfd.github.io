Master’s thesis · TUM · 2026

Flow over Piano Key Weirs

How do weir geometry and downstream bed shape influence turbulent flow and bed shear stress?

OpenFOAMVOFk–ω SST

I investigated four configurations combining linear and non-linear outlet keys with flat and experimentally reconstructed scoured beds. The workflow covered meshing, parallel simulations, validation and time-averaged flow analysis.

Methods and findings

Discharge-coefficient differences from experiments were 7.35% and 4.20% for the flat-bed cases. The prescribed scour cavities produced recirculating flow and lower overall bed shear stresses within the cavities. Sediment transport and evolving erosion were not simulated directly.

Thesis grade: 1.3 in the German grading system. Host: TUM Chair of Hydraulic Engineering.

CFD → Neural networkScalar concentration predictionPROJECT 02
Collaborative project · BAYER GLIP

Learning turbulent micromixing

Predicting scalar concentration fields using neural networks and CFD-generated turbulence data.

TensorFlowPythonScalar transport

Our team investigated neural-network approaches to micromixing in homogeneous isotropic turbulence, connecting scalar-transport simulation data with data-driven prediction.

Project approach

The report explores fully connected and convolutional networks, including a 3D U-Net, with concentration non-negativity and smoothness penalties. My experience included data preparation and neural-network implementation, training and validation.

Collaborative academic work with Usama Khalid, Aly Morsy and Busayomi Thompson-Ajayi, with Elin Vesper and Daniele Marchisio.

Liquid + gasTransient tank sloshingPROJECT 03
Collaborative project · TUM · 2026

Sloshing in a storage tank

Investigating moving liquid interfaces, wall pressures and thermal behaviour under harmonic excitation.

ANSYS FluentTransient VOFHeat transfer

A two-dimensional, partially filled tank study using water and two surrogate gases. The team examined volume fraction, velocity, pressure and temperature to characterise the transient response.

Modelling scope

The model combines VOF, k–ω SST and the energy equation under sinusoidal acceleration. The project includes a mesh-resolution comparison using average upper-wall pressure. Actual hydrogen was not modelled.

Oil / WaterGravity-driven separationPROJECT 04
Collaborative project · 2024

Oil–water gravity separation

Exploring how mesh resolution and modelling choices affect liquid–liquid flow predictions.

ANSYS FluentMultiphase CFD

With Busayomi David Thompson-Ajayi, I studied phase distribution and velocity fields in a horizontal gravity separator, comparing mesh resolutions and exploring alternative inlet arrangements.

What the study examined

The report compares coarse, medium and fine meshes and discusses laminar, k–ε and k–ω modelling choices. It is an exploratory academic simulation study rather than an experimentally validated separator design.
